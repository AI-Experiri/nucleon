# 0006 — Flagship pivot: Qwen3.8-27B (2026-08-14)

The user asked whether a model released today, dictated as
"2.827P GGF", would run on our planned dense-Qwen3 work. Research
identified it as Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B (family qwen3_5, the
Qwen3-Next/hybrid lineage) and the answer was no: 48 of 64 layers are
Gated DeltaNet linear attention with a recurrent+conv cache; the rest
are gated GQA with partial RoPE at head_dim 256.

Decision (user): Qwen3.8-27B replaces DeepSeek-V2-Lite as the M4
flagship. DeepSeek stays a later-family candidate; its research doc
remains. Qwen3-0.6B dense stays as the bring-up mule (fast iteration,
one unknown at a time). bf16 Metal compute becomes a hard M2
requirement (27.8B does not fit in 128 GB at f32).

New research doc: docs/research/qwen38-27b.md (config-verified facts +
sources). Plan, README, and the book's Part III updated. Process note:
the first attempt at this update half-applied because a scripted patch
ran despite a failed assertion — the patch runner now stops the whole
chain on any failure.
